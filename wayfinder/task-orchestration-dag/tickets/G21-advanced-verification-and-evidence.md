# G21 — Advanced verification and evidence providers

**Type**: grilling
**Status**: open
**Blocked by**: [G20 First-release scope, compatibility, and evaluation](G20-v1-scope-and-evaluation.md)
**Blocks**: —

## Question

How should verification grow beyond the first release's reusable criteria without turning the Plan DAG into a general evaluation platform?

Decide domain criteria, composed and asynchronous verification, reusable evidence stores, LLM judges, assurance policy, verifier versioning, timeout and inconclusive results, security, and community verifier packages. Extract a separate verification capability only after another independent consumer or provider-lifecycle need exists.
